Eravikulam National Park

Eravikulam National Park is a protected wildlife reserve located in the Western Ghats of Kerala. Famous for its rolling grasslands, misty hills, and rich biodiversity, the park is home to the endangered Nilgiri Tahr and offers breathtaking panoramic views of nature.

Scenic Views of Eravikulam National Park

Visitors: Thousands of tourists visit Eravikulam National Park every year, especially during the blooming season of Neelakurinji flowers and peak holiday periods.

About Eravikulam National Park

Eravikulam National Park is one of Kerala’s most treasured natural reserves, known for its scenic beauty, rolling hills, and diverse wildlife. Located near Munnar, the park plays a vital role in conservation and eco-tourism, attracting visitors from across the world.
