Anamudi Peak

Anamudi Peak, the highest peak in South India, is located in the Western Ghats within Eravikulam National Park near Munnar. Standing tall at 2,695 meters, it offers breathtaking views, rich biodiversity, and a paradise for nature lovers.

Scenic Views of Anamudi Peak

Visitors: Anamudi Peak attracts nature enthusiasts, trekkers, and wildlife lovers. Entry is regulated to protect the fragile ecosystem, making guided visits essential.

About Anamudi Peak

Anamudi Peak is a natural treasure of Kerala and an integral part of the Western Ghats. It plays a vital role in maintaining ecological balance and offers a unique experience of high-altitude biodiversity and scenic beauty.
