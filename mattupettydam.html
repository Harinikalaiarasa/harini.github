Mattupetty Dam

Mattupetty Dam is one of the most popular tourist attractions in Munnar, located about 13 km from Munnar town. Surrounded by lush green hills, tea plantations, and misty landscapes, it is a perfect place for nature lovers and adventure seekers.

About Mattupetty Dam

Mattupetty Dam is a beautiful tourist destination near Munnar, Kerala, famous for its serene lake, lush green hills, and scenic landscapes. It is an ideal place for boating, photography, and nature lovers.
