Tata Tea Museum

Tata Tea Museum, located in Munnar, showcases the rich heritage and history of tea plantations in Kerala. The museum offers insights into tea processing, vintage machinery, and the evolution of tea culture in the region.

Scenic Views of Tea Museum

Visitors: Tata Tea Museum attracts thousands of visitors every year, including tourists, tea enthusiasts, and students interested in the history of tea plantations.

About Tea Museum

Tata Tea Museum preserves the legacy of tea plantations in Munnar and highlights the role of tea in shaping the region’s economy and culture. It serves as an educational and cultural landmark for visitors.
