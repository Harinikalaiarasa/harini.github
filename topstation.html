Top Station

Top Station is one of the most breathtaking viewpoints in Munnar, Famous for its panoramic views of the Western Ghats, this spot offers a stunning sight of mist-covered valleys, rolling hills, and deep green landscapes.It is especially popular for sunrise and sunset views, when the sky changes colors and clouds float below the viewpoint.

Scenic Views of Top Station

Visitors: Top Station is a very popular viewpoint visited by both domestic and international tourists throughout the year. All day ( best sunrise at early morning ). Best months to visit September to March ( cool & clear views).

About Top Station

Located about 32km from Munnar Town on the Munnar-Kodaikannal road. Stunning views of the Western Ghats, Theni Valley, and clouds rolling over the hills.Early morning for clear views or April-May for pleasant weather avoid peak monsoon.
