Attukad Waterfalls

Attukad Waterfalls is one of the most beautiful and serene waterfalls in Munnar.Surrounded by lush green hills, dense forests, and tea plantations, the waterfall offers a refreshing escape into nature.The waterfall is at its best during the monsoon season, when water flows powerfully down the rocky slopes, creating a spectacular view. The cool mist, chirping birds, and scenic surroundings make it a perfect spot for nature lovers, photographers, and short nature walks.

Scenic Views of Attukad Waterfalls

Visitors: Enjoy scenic views, trekking through lush greenery and tea plantations, picnicking, photography, and experiencing the cool, refreshing air, especially during monsoon/winter when the falls are fuller
